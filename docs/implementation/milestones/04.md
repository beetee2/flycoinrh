## Delivered behavior

[ARENA](../ARENA.md) records the frozen rules and limitations:

- A 96×96 scene with two separated patterned pads, a lower start and zero through
  three approved solid distractions. Fixed geometry leaves routes to both pads.
- Default cohort rewards stripes; checkerboard rewards require an explicit
  separate cohort. Destination-side metadata must agree with the chosen cue.
- Pure motion at 8 pixels/axis/tick, quantized to 1/256 pixel, with exact rational
  swept contacts. First contact stops both axes. Click and neural time do not
  change scoring. Pad contact produces success/wrong-pad; the 256th tick otherwise
  produces trial_timeout. Terminal states cannot advance.
- One immutable grayscale raster. Lossless PNG decodes to identical bytes.
  A 128-pixel field samples to the existing 16×16 observation, with nearest cell
  centers, edge clipping and normalization by 255. Digests hash raw pixel bytes.
- Rendering accepts visible Scene only. Scoring and overlays stay outside it.
  The actual adapter on a labeled synthetic graph emits identical serialized
  outputs under different cohort scoring when pixels/checkpoint/seed are equal.

Legal geometry prevents simultaneous two-pad or solid/pad ties; ordering is
nevertheless explicit: solids before pads, then left before right. The reachable
pad-versus-timeout tie is directly tested. Large segment tests cross entire
pads/obstacles to validate first-contact math. Other cases cover wall clipping,
obstacle tangency/escape, diagonal/corner contact and nonfinite input rejection.
Seeded fixture closed-loop traces reproduce exact state and observation hashes.

Nearest sampling can alias narrow obstacles/pattern cells. This is a declared
condition for 05's visual-sensitivity measurements, not evidence of perception.
Closed scoring versus half-open raster fill means a terminal point can appear
one pixel beyond a pad's lower/right pattern boundary; the convention is explicit.
